# Contributor License Agreement

## Why

NeuroRouter is developed by Obsta Labs, LLC and uses an AGPL community edition plus commercial licensing for organizations that need different terms. To maintain this, all contributions must grant rights that allow both licensing tracks to coexist.

Without a CLA, outside contributions can only be distributed under the open-source license, which limits the project's ability to offer commercial terms.

## Agreement

You agree to the following by commenting `I have read the CLA and I agree` on your first pull request, as described under **How to sign** below:

1. **You own the work.** You represent that the contribution is your original work, or you have the right to submit it under the terms of this agreement.

2. **Grant of rights.** You grant Obsta Labs, LLC a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable license to use, reproduce, modify, distribute, sublicense, and otherwise exploit your contribution in any form, including under commercial license terms.

3. **Patent grant.** You grant Obsta Labs, LLC a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable patent license to make, use, sell, and distribute your contribution and derivative works.

4. **No obligation.** Obsta Labs, LLC is not obligated to use your contribution.

5. **You keep your rights.** You retain all rights to your contribution. This agreement does not transfer ownership — it grants a license.

## How to sign

Comment `I have read the CLA and I agree` on your first pull request. That comment is the moment you enter this agreement, and it covers all your future contributions to this repository.

A bot checks each pull request for that comment and posts instructions if it is missing. Opening a pull request on its own does not sign anything.
